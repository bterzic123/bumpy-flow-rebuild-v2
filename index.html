What this is

We searched the way a new user does, then rebuilt what happens after the tap.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. For Bumpy we looked at two things. First, your app: we installed it and walked every step from your name to Gold. Second, your Apple Ads: which searches you show up on, which you win, and where you barely register.

What follows is five ideas for the flow you have, and two keyword flows that show what happens when each search gets its own version. Every one is a hypothesis you can A/B test, and all of it is clickable. The prototype uses your design: your teal gradient, white cards, emoji pills, intro video and Gold paywall.
